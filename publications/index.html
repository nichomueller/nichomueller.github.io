Reduced basis solvers for unfitted methods on parameterised domains

N. Mueller, S. Badia, Y. Zhao. Computer Methods in Applied Mechanics and Engineering, 2026.

Geometry that changes shape is one of the more annoying things to handle in reduced-order modelling, since it usually means remeshing every time. This combines unfitted methods with reduced basis ideas to avoid that.

2026 · Preprint

Opal.jl: a comprehensive, composable framework for data assimilation in Julia

N. Mueller. Submitted.

Sequential Bayesian filters and variational estimators behind one Julia package, with localisation, inflation, bias correction, and integration with ROM pipelines for efficiency. Tested on Lorenz-63, a Van der Pol oscillator with biased observations, Reynolds-number discovery in a turbulent Navier-Stokes flow, and a heat equation running on a reduced-order surrogate instead of the full model.

Experience

Work that connects method development to application

2026 - present

Research Fellow, TU Delft

Developing digital-twin and large-scale multiphysics simulation frameworks for offshore floating solar energy systems.

  • Building the forecasting and uncertainty quantification layer of a digital ocean model that predicts wave, wind, and structural loads across a floating solar farm.
  • Feeding directly into design decisions for offshore renewable energy infrastructure.
  • Competitive research fellowship within DigiOcean4Solar, supported by the VIDI scheme.
  • Built Opals.jl, an open-source data assimilation package spanning Kalman/particle filters and 4D-Var across SciML and Gridap/GridapROMs backends. Its reservoir-computing module for bias correction beats ReservoirComputing.jl, the standard Julia RC library, on both accuracy and training time.
2022 - 2025

PhD Researcher, Monash University

Reduced-order methods and scientific software for parameterised PDEs, mostly aimed at problems too large for the textbook approach to survive.

  • Built methods spanning space-time reduction, tensor decompositions, unfitted finite elements, and scalable HPC workflows.
  • Authored GridapROMs.jl, an open-source Julia library for reduced-order modeling; its algorithms have achieved up to 100,000x speedups over full-order simulations.
2020 - 2021

R&D Intern, CSEM

Topology optimisation software for compliant aerospace mechanisms, built against real compliance and stress constraints from the manufacturing side.
